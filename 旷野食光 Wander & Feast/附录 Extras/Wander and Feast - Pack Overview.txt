================================================================
Wander & Feast
Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge
================================================================

    What the player meets first is a rearranged landscape. Tectonic gives
the ridgelines real layering, cuts river valleys through the plateaus and
lets peaks rise under the cloud line. Biomes come from Biomes O' Plenty and
vanilla together: maple woods, lavender fields, hot springs, jacaranda
glades, redwood forests and seasonal woodland on one map. Villages and
temples are joined by roads laid down by RoadWeaver, with the ten YUNG
structure overhauls - dungeons, mineshafts, strongholds, ocean monuments,
jungle and desert temples, witch huts, nether fortresses, end islands and
villages - distributed along them; Better Village and TerraBlender handle
village placement and biome transition. Back at the house the rhythm changes
entirely: the Kaleidoscope stove, steamer, wok and grill, the Farmer's
Delight pot and its recipes, the maids on the veranda, the orchard and
fields of Biomes O' Plenty, Alex's Mobs and Alex's Delight carrying game
into the kitchen, Butchery Lite breaking carcasses into materials. Travel is
handled by Automobility cars, Fish and Ships sailboats and trawlers,
Immersive Aircraft; ranged combat by Launchers And Arrows; supply by Lite
Exchange. On screen, Sodium, Sodium Extra, Iris, Photon shaders and Voxy LOD
carry the picture, with Lithium, FerriteCore, ModernFix and a set of fix
mods underneath. These mods are not a pile of separate parts; in the same
world they connect to one another.

    You follow a stone-brick path into the valley. Redwood trunks stand on
either side, sunlight comes down through the leaves and lays narrow shadows
across the bricks. At the end of the path is a village, its roof tiles new,
the waystone at the gate not yet lit; farther in, a mineshaft mouth opens in
the slope, old rope hanging from its timber frame, the shaft dropping into
black. You pull the map back and the roads join into a net - north to the
snowfields, east to the volcano, south to a valley full of jacaranda. At
dusk you are home: the stove is still burning, the steamer is throwing white
steam, a maid is gathering vegetables in the yard, and the fish on the rack
catch a film of oil in the firelight. Tomorrow you mean to walk the path you
have not walked yet, and see where it goes.

    The pack ships in three editions:

    Wander & Feast: Homesteaded      110 mods
    Wander & Feast: Expedition       107 mods
    Wander & Feast: Confluence       121 mods

----------------------------------------------------------------
Wander & Feast: Homesteaded
----------------------------------------------------------------

    This edition draws the world back to the yard and the stove. Tectonic
tightens the terrain: ridges no longer run straight into the clouds, and the
valleys and gentle slopes are left for fields and houses. Biomes O' Plenty
disables the grimmer and more intrusive biomes and keeps what can be farmed,
lived in or looked at, with biomes packed closer together so that a few
minutes of walking takes you from maple woods to lavender fields. Structures
are not tuned for thrills; villages are the main landmarks, and mineshafts
and dungeons stay nearby without demanding a descent. Cooking is the spine of
the edition: Kaleidoscope Cookery, Chinese Food, Orchard, Tavern, End and
Nether form a complete production line - the orchard supplies, the tavern
absorbs, the maids restock and serve. Cars and boats are for carrying the
harvest home, not for long journeys. It suits players who want to live out
their days in one place.

Wander & Feast: Expedition
----------------------------------------------------------------

    This edition pushes the rhythm out onto the road. Tectonic keeps its
higher cap and greater relief: peaks, ravines, volcanoes, wastelands and cold
deserts all remain, and the elevation itself is the reason to travel. Biomes
O' Plenty keeps every biome, so a single journey can pass through maple
woods, redwood forests, hot springs and cold desert. The ten YUNG structure
overhauls are denser and more widely spread - mineshafts, strongholds, ocean
monuments, jungle and desert temples, witch huts, nether fortresses, end
islands and villages form a net across the map, with RoadWeaver laying roads
between them so that driving has direction. Combat and hunting are the
spine: Alex's Mobs adds a large roster of creatures, Alex's Delight carries
game into the kitchen, Butchery Lite breaks it down, and Launchers And Arrows
adds longbows, ballistae and more than ten kinds of special bolts. Vehicles
cover three terrains - cars, sailboats and trawlers, aircraft. Kaleidoscope
and the maid series are not included; lighter luggage travels further.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Wander & Feast: Confluence
----------------------------------------------------------------

    This edition puts the content of both split editions into one world, with
full parameters: every Biomes O' Plenty biome, Tectonic at its highest cap
and relief, Kaleidoscope and Farmer's Delight kitchens side by side, the maid
series and Alex series together, cars, sailboats and aircraft all available.
It is not a good way to find out which playstyle you prefer - carrying both
content sets at once, the rhythm keeps switching between cooking at home and
hunting far afield, the density is higher and the tone more mixed. Its
purpose is to give players who will not give up any content an option; the
two split editions were cut from it.

    Mod list: everything in Homesteaded and Expedition, plus the mods unique
    to each of those editions.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Shared by all three editions
----------------------------------------------------------------

    The foundation is identical, so switching editions requires no
relearning of controls. Rendering is carried by Sodium, Sodium Extra, Iris,
Voxy and Voxy World Gen, with Lithium, FerriteCore, ModernFix, Dynamic FPS,
EntityCulling, ImmediatelyFast, MoreCulling, AllTheLeaks, PacketFixer and
K Multi Threading underneath; five shader packs ship in the shaderpacks
folder - Bliss, BSL, Complementary Reimagined, Photon and Solas. For tools,
JEI handles recipes, Xaero's minimap and world map handle navigation,
Forgematica ships with nine ready-made schematics, and Waystones link bases.
Multiplayer runs on Simple Voice Chat with no extra software. The Chinese
translation comes as three resource packs: the base pack covers 49
namespaces, the homestead pack 8, the expedition pack 18. Mods that already
provide Chinese are left alone and the packs only fill the gaps; Confluence
needs all three, the split editions two each.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Hardware
----------------------------------------------------------------

    Voxy, shaders and Voxygen are all off by default, which keeps the pack
friendly to low-end machines. It still has a lot of headroom: if the hardware
allows, turning all three on is recommended, and it changes the visuals
dramatically.

    Minimum
        Quad-core CPU, 8 GB RAM (4-6 GB allocated to the game),
        integrated graphics or entry-level discrete GPU, HDD acceptable,
        shaders and LOD off, render distance 6-8, simulation distance 5-6.

    Recommended
        Six-core CPU or better, 16 GB RAM (6-8 GB allocated),
        RTX 3050 or better, SSD,
        Voxy LOD and light shaders on, render distance 10-12.

    Full
        Eight-core CPU or better, 32 GB RAM (8-10 GB allocated),
        RTX 4060 or better, NVMe SSD,
        Photon shaders, Voxy LOD and Voxygen all on, render distance 16 or
        more; LOD pregeneration and terrain detail maxed, Photon clouds,
        volumetric light and shadows fully enabled.
