[About this first release]

    0.9 is the first public baseline, not a finished product. The goal for
this version is narrow: fix the mod list, fill in the Chinese translation,
make the pack enterable and playable, and then listen to feedback. World
generation, structure density, loot and shader parameters are still at their
defaults; only the terrain and biome changes confirmed in this round have
been applied, and the three editions have not yet been retuned separately.

    Two things are worth stating up front: the three editions use different
terrain parameters, so a save moved between them will lose content, and
Homesteaded and Expedition are not meant to share a server. If the game
crashes or a value feels wrong, note the edition name and the exact 
symptom. We would greatly appreciate it.

[Positioning]

    All three editions share the same foundation and differ only in content
direction. Homesteaded draws the world back to the house and the yard;
Expedition spreads it out onto the road and into the wilds; Confluence keeps
both content sets for players who will not give up anything. The two split
editions are cut from Confluence.

    Wander & Feast: Homesteaded      110 mods
    Wander & Feast: Expedition       107 mods
    Wander & Feast: Confluence       121 mods

[File list]

introductions are not distributed inside the modpack archives.

    "整合包 Modpacks" holds the two split editions and is the recommended
download. "附录 Extras" holds Confluence and the two introductions.
Confluence is not recommended as a first choice, which is why it sits in the
appendix rather than the main folder.

[Archive structure]

    Each zip is an importable modpack with the same layout:

    modrinth.index.json    mod list with download checksums
    overrides/             files copied over the instance directory

    overrides contains:

    mods/                  the mods themselves: 110 files (Homesteaded),
                           107 (Expedition), 122 (Confluence)
    config/                mod configuration: 315 / 309 / 309 files
    shaderpacks/           five packs: Bliss, BSL, Complementary Reimagined,
                           Photon, Solas
    resourcepacks/         SodiumTranslations, Jinhehuan's Masa pack
                           translations, Jinhehuan's Xaero map translations
    global_packs/          new recipe datapack (optional_data, toggleable at
                           world creation)
    packmenu/              PackMenu main-menu configuration
    PCL/                   PCL launcher personalisation files
    options.txt            video, keybind and language settings

[Chinese translation]

    Three resource packs: base, homestead and expedition. Mods that already
provide Chinese are left alone; the packs only fill the gaps.

    旷野食光：基础汉化.zip     49 namespaces
    旷野食光：栖居汉化.zip      8 namespaces
    旷野食光：远行汉化.zip     18 namespaces

    Confluence needs all three; Homesteaded and Expedition each need two
(base plus their own). The packs are not distributed inside the modpack
archives and sit in the modpack folder separately.
